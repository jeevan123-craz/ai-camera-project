MEDGUARD CLINIC - KNOWLEDGE BASE / FAQs

1. Operating Hours:
- Monday to Friday: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
- Saturday: 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM
- Sunday: Closed

2. Accepted Insurance:
- We accept BlueCross, Medicare, Aetna, Cigna, and UnitedHealthcare.
- We DO NOT accept Medicaid or out-of-state HMOs.

3. Pricing & Fees:
- General Consultation (without insurance): $150
- Specialist Consultation: $250
- No-show fee: $50 if canceled less than 24 hours in advance.

4. Location & Parking:
- Address: 123 Health Ave, Medical District, NY 10001
- Parking: Free validated parking is available in the garage behind the building. Bring your ticket to the front desk.

5. Walk-ins vs Appointments:
- We strongly recommend booking an appointment. Walk-ins are accepted but expect a 1-2 hour wait time depending on doctor availability.

6. Pharmacy:
- We have an on-site pharmacy that is open during clinic hours for your convenience.
